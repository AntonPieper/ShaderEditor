# Examples

Each example maps one group of inputs to a picture you can check by looking. The drawer lists them under these headings; open any file to see how an example is built.

## Start here
- [Shapes](Shapes.frag) Touch the screen; the orbit moves to your finger
- [Loop](Loop.frag) Rotate to restart; each hand loops at its own rate
- [Touch](Touch.frag) How to process touch input
- [Texture](Texture.frag) How to draw textures

## Device inputs
- [Tilt](Tilt.frag) Tilt the phone; the ball rolls to the low side
- [Compass](Compass.frag) Hold it flat and turn; the needle points north
- [Window](Window.frag) Turn around; the sky stays put behind the glass
- [Meters](Meters.frag) Cover the phone's top; the left and right bars drop
- [Battery](Battery.frag) Plug in the charger; the level rises in pulses
- [Clock](Clock.frag) Compare with the status bar; the hands agree
- [Sound](Sound.frag) Make a sound; the ring swells (microphone)
- [Notifications](Notifications.frag) Get a notification; a dot appears (notifications)
- [Back camera](Back%20camera.frag) Turn the phone; the view stays upright (camera)
- [Mirror](Mirror.frag) Raise your right hand; it is on the right (camera)

## Techniques
- [Trails](Trails.frag) Draw with a finger; the stroke fades behind it
- [Game of Life](Game%20of%20Life/) Conway's Game of Life. Touch to spark new life.
- [Electric Fade](Electric%20Fade.frag) Touch to spark life; dead cells leave a glow
- [Swirl](Swirl.frag) Drag across the image; it twists where you began
- [GLES 3](GLES%203.frag) Wait a moment; hashed tiles turn their paths
- [Blobs](Blobs.frag) Drag a finger; its blob melts into the others
- [Skybox](Skybox.frag) Turn around; the sun stays in the east
- [Bloom](Bloom/) Only the brightest lamps glow; touch to add one

## Wallpapers
- [Horizon](Horizon.frag) Swipe home screen pages; near hills move most
- [Ink](Ink/) Tilt the phone; the ink drifts downhill
